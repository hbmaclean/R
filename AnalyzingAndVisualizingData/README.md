# Analyzing and Visualizing Data

Here I intend to analyze various data sets to exercise my recent Data Science certification and introduction to R.  My primary goal is to continue learning.  Achieving high accuracy or low RMSE will be a bit of a game.

I start with two data sets from University of California Irvine:

* [UCI Leaf](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/288/leaf) - predicting plant species from 15 shape and texture attributes

* [UCI Mushroom](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/73/mushroom) - predicting poisonousness based on cap, gill, stem, root, odor, habitat, and other features
