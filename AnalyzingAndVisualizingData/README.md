# Analyzing and Visualizing Data

Here I intend to analyze various data sets to exercise my recent Data Science certification and introduction to R.  My primary goal is to continue learning.  Achieving high accuracy or low RMSE will be a bit of a game.

I started with two data sets from University of California Irvine and a third from Kaggle:

* [UCI Mushroom](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/73/mushroom) - predicting poisonousness based on cap, gill, stem, root, odor, habitat, and other features

* [UCI Leaf](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/288/leaf) - predicting plant species from 15 shape and texture attributes

* [Kaggle Plant Disease Classification](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/turakut/plant-disease-classification/) - predicting plant disease based on rainfall, humidity, temperature, and soil pH

After progressing in that order and making incremental improvements, I created data.frame.X, a package to house repeated patterns.  Future development may inform a more appropriate name.

Next I intend to analyze another data set or few, using and improving data.frame.X.
